Questions Answered

Orillia Renovation FAQs

Do you serve waterfront properties on Lake Couchiching and Lake Simcoe?

Yes — waterfront and lakeside renovations are a regular part of our work in Orillia. We understand the additional considerations around moisture, seasonal access, and shoreline permitting that come with lake properties.

Do you handle permits with the City of Orillia?

Absolutely. We manage all permit applications and inspections required by the City of Orillia so your renovation is fully code-compliant from start to finish.

How far in advance should I book a renovation in Orillia?

We recommend reaching out 6-8 weeks before your desired start date, especially for kitchen and basement projects that involve custom cabinetry lead times. We'll give you an accurate timeline during your free consultation.

Can you work with older or century homes near downtown Orillia?

Yes — we have extensive experience updating character and century homes, balancing modern function with the architectural details that make these homes special.

Is there a travel charge for projects in Orillia?

No — Orillia is a core part of our Simcoe County service area, and estimates, consultations, and project pricing are the same as anywhere else we serve.
