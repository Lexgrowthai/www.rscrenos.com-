Questions Answered

Basement Finishing FAQs

Do I need a permit to finish my basement?

Yes — basement finishing almost always requires a building permit, especially when adding bedrooms, bathrooms, or a secondary suite. RSC Renos manages the full permit and inspection process for you.

Are egress windows required?

Any room used as a bedroom must have a code-compliant egress window for fire safety. We assess your existing window openings and install or enlarge them as needed to meet Ontario Building Code requirements.

How do you deal with moisture and water issues?

We start every basement project with a moisture assessment. Depending on conditions, we install interior drainage systems, vapor barriers, sump pumps, and proper grading before any framing begins — this step is never skipped.

How long does a basement renovation take?

A standard basement finish typically takes 6–10 weeks. Legal secondary suites with separate entrances and full kitchens can take 10–16 weeks depending on permit approval timelines.

What are the legal requirements for a secondary suite in Ontario?

Legal secondary suites require a separate entrance, adequate ceiling height, fire separation between units, egress windows, and full compliance with the Ontario Building Code and local zoning bylaws. We handle the entire compliance process.

What ceiling height do I need to finish my basement?

Ontario Building Code generally requires a minimum finished ceiling height of 6'5" (1.95m) for basement living space. We'll measure your joist height during the free consultation and advise on options if your space is tight.
