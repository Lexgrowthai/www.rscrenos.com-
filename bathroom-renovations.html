Questions Answered

Bathroom Renovation FAQs

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

Most full bathroom renovations take 2–4 weeks from demo to final walkthrough. Larger ensuites with structural changes or custom millwork can take longer — we give you a realistic, written timeline before work begins.

How do you prevent mold and moisture damage?

We use professional-grade waterproofing membranes behind all tile and shower surrounds, properly sized exhaust ventilation, and correctly sloped drains — all inspected before tile goes down, so moisture never becomes a hidden problem.

Is heated flooring worth the cost?

Most clients say it's the single feature they'd never renovate without again. In-floor radiant heat adds a modest cost to a tile installation but dramatically improves comfort, especially in Ontario winters.

Should I choose a walk-in shower or keep my tub?

It depends on your household — walk-in showers maximize accessibility and modern appeal, while at least one tub is often recommended if you have young children or plan to sell in the future. We'll help you weigh the trade-offs.

Do you offer aging-in-place or accessible bathroom designs?

Yes — curbless showers, grab bar blocking, comfort-height vanities, and non-slip flooring can all be built in from the start, so your bathroom stays functional and beautiful as your needs change.
