Questions Answered

Severn Renovation FAQs

Do you renovate cottages and seasonal properties, not just year-round homes?

Yes — cottage and seasonal-property renovations are a regular part of our work in Severn. We understand the additional considerations around moisture, seasonal access, and off-grid or well-and-septic systems that come with cottage-country properties.

Do you handle permits with Severn Township?

Absolutely. We manage all permit applications and inspections required by Severn Township so your renovation is fully code-compliant from start to finish.

How far in advance should I book a renovation in Severn?

We recommend reaching out 6-8 weeks before your desired start date, especially for kitchen and basement projects that involve custom cabinetry lead times. We'll give you an accurate timeline during your free consultation.

Can you work with older rural or farmhouse-style properties?

Yes — we have extensive experience updating rural and farmhouse-style homes, balancing modern function with the character that makes these properties special.

Is there a travel charge for projects in Severn?

No — Severn is a core part of our service area, and estimates, consultations, and project pricing are the same as anywhere else we serve.
