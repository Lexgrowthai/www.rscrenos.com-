Questions Answered

Kitchen Renovation FAQs

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

Most full kitchen renovations take 4–8 weeks from demo to final walkthrough, depending on scope, custom cabinetry lead times, and whether structural changes are involved. We give you a realistic, written timeline before work begins.

Do I need permits for my kitchen renovation?

Permits are typically required for electrical, plumbing relocation, or any structural/load-bearing changes. RSC Renovations handles all permit applications and inspections so your renovation is fully code-compliant.

Can I still use my kitchen during the renovation?

For most full renovations, your kitchen will be out of commission for part of the project. We help set up a temporary mini-kitchen where possible and keep disruption to a minimum with clear daily scheduling.

What's the difference between refacing and replacing cabinets?

Refacing keeps your existing cabinet boxes and replaces doors, drawer fronts, and hardware — a faster, more budget-friendly option. Full replacement changes the layout, materials, and storage entirely. We'll help you decide which makes sense for your goals.

Do you handle appliance selection and installation?

Yes — we can work with appliances you've already purchased, or help you select and source a package that fits your design, budget, and cooking style, with full professional installation included.

What if I don't know what style I want yet?

That's completely normal. Our design team will walk you through material samples, cabinet styles, and layout options during your free consultation to help you land on a direction you're excited about.
